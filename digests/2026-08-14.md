# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-08-14

## Top items

### 1. Microsoft SharePoint Auth Bypass (CVE-2026-55040) Actively Exploited After PoC Release
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Unauthenticated admin-level access to a platform that anchors document repositories and permissions across most enterprises — an immediate patch-compliance and exposure-scanning priority.
Attackers began exploiting CVE-2026-55040 (CVSS 9.1) within days of a public PoC, logging in as any user, including admins, without valid credentials. Researchers had disclosed the underlying exploit chain on Aug 11 using AI-assisted vulnerability research. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/attackers-exploit-sharepoint.html))

### 2. Cisco ASA/FTD Heap Flaw (CVE-2026-20349) Exploited — Federal Deadline Today
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Firewalls sit at the perimeter; a wild-exploited bug with a KEV-mandated remediation deadline of today is a direct compliance trigger and a segmentation-resilience test case.
CISA added CVE-2026-20349 (CVSS 8.6), a heap-inspection flaw in Cisco Secure Firewall ASA/FTD, to its KEV catalog on Aug 11 after confirming in-the-wild DoS exploitation; federal agencies must remediate by Aug 14. ([source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/08/11/cisa-adds-three-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog))

### 3. Microsoft August Patch Tuesday: 398 Flaws, One Kernel Zero-Day Under Attack
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Sets this month's patch-compliance baseline; the actively exploited kernel driver flaw is the top endpoint-remediation priority.
Microsoft's August updates addressed 398 vulnerabilities, including a Windows Ancillary Function Driver (WinSock) use-after-free already exploited in the wild plus two other publicly disclosed zero-days. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/microsoft-patches-398-flaws-including.html))

### 4. CISA Publishes 13 Siemens/OT Advisories in a Single Batch
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Broad, simultaneous OT/ICS disclosures across manufacturing and building-automation gear is a systematic-inventory trigger; several flaws are classic post-breach paths into production networks.
CISA released 13 advisories on Aug 13 covering Siemens Solid Edge, Siveillance Video, Simcenter Femap, Parasolid, License Server, LOGO! Soft Comfort, and Desigo controllers, plus AVEVA Enterprise SCADA and Johnson Controls products — spanning file-parsing RCE, privilege escalation, and BACnet DoS. ([source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-26-225-01))

### 5. Trezor Discloses Breach of ~14,000 Customers via Shipping Vendor
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A textbook third-party/vendor-risk failure — the compromise entered through a logistics partner, not Trezor itself, reinforcing why vendor access reviews belong in every audit scope.
Hardware wallet maker Trezor disclosed a breach affecting roughly 14,000 customers after its shipping and logistics provider, ShipMonk, was hacked, exposing contact details tied to crypto-hardware purchases. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/trezor-discloses-data-breach-affecting-nearly-14-000-customers/))

### 6. Microsoft Patches "LegacyHive" Windows Zero-Day Outside Normal Cycle
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** An out-of-band fix after public disclosure is a reactive-patching case study — useful for testing whether your org's emergency-patch SLA holds under real conditions.
Microsoft shipped an emergency patch for LegacyHive, a Windows zero-day disclosed after Patch Tuesday closed; details remain limited but the flaw enables elevated access on affected systems. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/microsoft-patches-legacyhive-windows-zero-day-vulnerability/))

### 7. New GeoServer SQL Injection Already Being Weaponized
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** GeoServer has a history of internet-facing exposure driving mass exploitation; if it's in your stack, this is an immediate external-scan-and-patch item.
Researcher Shi Hu Fang disclosed a straightforward SQL injection in GeoServer that SANS reports is already showing exploitation attempts, echoing past GeoServer bugs that spread quickly once public. ([source](https://isc.sans.edu/podcastdetail/10052))

### 8. Apple Issues New Threat Notifications for Mercenary Spyware Targeting
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Signals continued targeting of high-value individuals (executives, government staff); a prompt to confirm your exec-protection and mobile-threat-detection program covers this vendor notification channel.
Apple rolled out a new "Threat Notification" alert format warning users their iPhone was targeted by mercenary spyware, formalizing a vendor-to-user incident channel for state-linked surveillance tools. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/apple/apple-sends-new-threat-notification-alerts-over-mercenary-spyware-attacks/))
