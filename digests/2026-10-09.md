# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-10-09

## Top items

### 1. CISA/FBI: China-Linked Actors Ran a Portal Distributing Stolen Government and Healthcare Email
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A compromised vendor (Integrity Technology Group) became a pivot point distributing stolen mailbox access to multiple downstream actors — a direct test of vendor account segmentation and third-party access reviews.
CISA, the FBI, and six partner nations detailed a China-linked campaign that stole email from government, law enforcement, healthcare, and religious organizations across Southeast Asia and resold access through a dedicated portal. ([source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa26-281a))

### 2. FBI Removes Accenture Contractor After Unpatched Flaw Enabled ShinyHunters Breach of FBI Staff Data
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A documented case of a contractor's missed patch window leading to a federal data breach — concrete evidence for SLA and patch-timeline clauses in vendor contracts.
The FBI removed an Accenture contractor after a failure to patch a known vulnerability on a contractor-managed system let the ShinyHunters group steal personal data on thousands of FBI employees. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/fbi-removes-accenture-contractor-after.html))

### 3. SonicWall Patches CVSS 10.0 Pre-Auth SSRF in SMA1000 Remote-Access Appliances
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** An unauthenticated SSRF on an internet-facing remote-access gateway is a same-day emergency-patch item; exploitation reaches internal systems with no credentials.
SonicWall shipped hotfixes for four SMA1000 flaws, the worst a maximum-severity SSRF letting an unauthenticated attacker route requests through the appliance into internal functions. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/sonicwall-patches-cvss-100-pre.html))

### 4. Tensorlake npm Package Compromised to Spread Shai-Hulud Credential-Stealing Worm
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A live supply-chain compromise in a widely-used SDK is a direct SBOM and dependency-scanning finding for any build pipeline pulling the package.
The "tensorlake" TypeScript SDK's version 0.5.144 was found to contain obfuscated malware from the ChainDrop/Shai-Hulud campaign that steals credentials from build environments. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/tensorlake-npm-package-compromised-to.html))

### 5. Commercial AI Pentesting Tool "ARTEX" Weaponized Against South Korean Financial Firms
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Legitimate offensive-security tooling used by a threat actor blurs the line between sanctioned testing and attack traffic — a case for stricter pentest authorization controls and behavioral monitoring.
CrowdStrike attributes a campaign against South Korean financial organizations, active since late 2026, to misuse of ARTEX, a commercial AI-driven pentesting platform, to steal data. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/artex-ai-pentesting-tool-used-in-data.html))

### 6. JPCERT: Sharp Rise in Japanese Web Data Leaks via Mobile API Abuse and Metabase Flaws
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** JPCERT ties the surge to API misconfiguration and delayed patching of known issues — a direct prompt for API exposure testing and patch-cadence review.
JPCERT/CC reported an increase in data leaks at Japanese organizations driven by attackers abusing mobile-app API misconfigurations and exploiting known Metabase flaws left unpatched. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/japan-sees-sharp-rise-in-web-data-leaks.html))

### 7. Wazza Phishkit Builds Evasion Infrastructure Into Phishing Pages Targeting Banking and Government
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Phishing kits now embed filtering, session management, and traffic control at the delivery layer — board-level relevant as it raises detection difficulty for high-value targets.
ANY.RUN documented the Wazza kit targeting banking, government, and manufacturing sectors across the US, EU, and Australia with built-in anti-detection controls, not just copied login pages. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/wazza-phishkit-targets-banking.html))
