# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-08-28

## Top items

### 1. PaperCut Zero-Day Actively Exploited, All NG and MF Versions Affected
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Print servers are frequently out of security scope yet hold domain credentials and network access; active zero-day exploitation with emergency patches demands immediate inclusion in patch-management and asset inventories.
PaperCut warned customers that attackers are actively exploiting a zero-day flaw in all versions of PaperCut NG and MF print-management software, and shipped emergency patches for v25 and v26. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/papercut-zero-day-exploited-in-attacks.html))

### 2. Critical cPanel Flaw Lets a Hosting Customer Take Root Control of the Server
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** In shared-hosting environments this collapses tenant isolation entirely — one low-privilege customer account can pivot to full server compromise, a key control audit teams must verify is patched.
cPanel patched CVE-2026-65643, a flaw in domain-parking/addon-domain functionality in cPanel and WHM that allows root-level code execution; all supported versions are affected. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/critical-cpanel-flaw-could-let-one.html))

### 3. CISA Adds Three More Flaws to KEV: ownCloud, Linux Kernel, JFrog Artifactory
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Two-day federal remediation deadlines for the ownCloud and Linux kernel bugs signal active, urgent exploitation; the Artifactory path-traversal flaw is a direct CI/CD supply-chain integrity risk.
CISA added CVE-2023-49105 (ownCloud improper authentication), CVE-2026-53362 (Linux kernel), and CVE-2026-66384 (JFrog Artifactory path traversal) to KEV, with federal deadlines of 2026-08-30 and 2026-09-10. ([source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/08/27/cisa-adds-three-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog))

### 4. APT28-Linked HOOKEDGE Backdoor Targets European Government and Diplomatic Bodies
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** State-sponsored targeting of government/diplomatic entities raises geopolitical risk and should prompt review of incident-response playbooks and hardening for comparable public-sector systems.
Recorded Future's Insikt Group flagged campaigns against government and diplomatic organizations in Romania, Spain, and Türkiye deploying a previously undocumented backdoor linked to APT28. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/apt28-linked-hookedge-backdoor-targets.html))

### 5. Next.js Patches Critical AVIF and Path-Traversal Flaws Enabling Unauthenticated RCE
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Next.js is widely deployed; unauthenticated RCE via crafted image uploads or path traversal is a common real-world attack surface that VAPT engagements should retest immediately.
Vercel patched two critical-severity Next.js vulnerabilities — one exploitable via crafted AVIF image files, the other via a Windows path-traversal flaw — both allowing unauthenticated remote code execution. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/nextjs-patches-critical-avif-and.html))

### 6. ServiceNow Patches Three Max-Severity AI Platform Vulnerabilities
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** ServiceNow underpins enterprise workflow and ITSM processes; max-severity code injection, SQL injection, and privilege-escalation bugs in its AI Platform warrant urgent patch verification.
ServiceNow released fixes for three maximum-severity AI Platform vulnerabilities exploitable for code injection, SQL injection, and privilege escalation. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/servicenow-warns-of-three-max-severity-security-vulnerabilities/))

### 7. Alleged TeamPCP Hackers Charged in Australia Over Supply-Chain Attacks
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Formal charges over the compromise of Trivy, Checkmarx KICS, and an AI gateway project confirm law-enforcement focus on developer-tooling supply-chain attacks — relevant for third-party and open-source dependency risk programs.
The Australian Federal Police charged two men with 14 offences tied to TeamPCP, the group behind the March 2026 compromise of open-source security scanners Trivy and Checkmarx KICS. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/alleged-teampcp-hackers-charged-in.html))

### 8. Carhartt Data Breach Exposes 12.9 Million Accounts
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A large-scale extortion-driven data leak is a reminder to verify third-party data-handling controls and breach-notification readiness ahead of similar disclosure obligations.
The ShinyHunters extortion group published data from nearly 13 million Carhartt accounts, per Have I Been Pwned. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/carhartt-data-breach-exposes-information-of-129-million-accounts/))
