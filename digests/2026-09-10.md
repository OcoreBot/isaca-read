# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-09-10

## Top items

### 1. CISA Adds Four KEV Entries — Citrix NetScaler, Cisco FMC, and Fortinet Flaws Under Active Exploitation
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Three enterprise network/security appliances now carry federal remediation deadlines of 2026-09-12 — a hard compliance checkpoint for any org running these products, federal or not.
CISA added CVE-2025-25249 (Fortinet heap buffer overflow), CVE-2026-19490 (Citrix NetScaler auth bypass), and CVE-2026-20079 (Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center auth bypass) to its KEV catalog on 2026-09-09. Cisco has separately confirmed CVE-2026-20079 is being actively exploited against FMC's management plane. ([source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/09/cisa-adds-four-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog))

### 2. Chrome V8 Zero-Day (CVE-2026-87491) Exploited, Added to KEV
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** The seventh actively exploited Chrome zero-day this year — sandbox-escape bugs in a near-universally deployed browser mean every unpatched endpoint is a live exposure, not a theoretical one.
Google's September update fixed 230 flaws, including an out-of-bounds write in V8 already under attack. CISA added it to KEV the same day, with federal remediation due 2026-09-23. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/google-patches-seventh-chrome-zero-day-exploited-in-attacks-this-year/))

### 3. AdaptHealth Confirms 4.1 Million People Exposed in July Breach
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A two-month gap between the July intrusion and this week's disclosure is itself an audit finding — breach-to-notification timelines are a direct test of incident-response and legal-review maturity.
Healthcare company AdaptHealth confirmed 4.1 million individuals' data was exposed in a July attack attributed to ShinyHunters, disclosed only on 2026-09-09. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/adapthealth-confirms-41-million-people-exposed-in-july-cyberattack/))

### 4. Veradigm Discloses Patient Data Breach After Vendor Ransomware Attack
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A third-party vendor compromise exposing patient data is a textbook supply-chain/vendor-risk control gap for healthcare audit scopes.
Healthcare technology company Veradigm disclosed a breach after a ransomware incident at a third-party vendor exposed patients' personal data. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/veradigm-discloses-patient-data-breach-after-gentlemen-gang-claims-attack/))

### 5. Microsoft Defender "ShieldCrash" PoC Bypasses Emergency ShieldBreak Patch
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Shows a rushed emergency fix was incomplete — orgs that patched CVE-2026-69414 and considered it closed remain exposed to SYSTEM-level escalation.
Researcher Chaotic Eclipse released a PoC (dubbed ShieldCrash) that bypasses Microsoft's patch for the ShieldBreak Defender flaw, granting SYSTEM access. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-microsoft-defender-shieldcrash-zero-day-grants-system-access/))

### 6. cPanel EmailTrack Flaw Lets Mail-Privileged Accounts Run Code as Root
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Privilege escalation from a low-tier hosting account to root undermines multi-tenant isolation assumptions that shared-hosting audits typically rely on.
cPanel patched a flaw letting an authenticated account with mail privileges create arbitrary files via EmailTrack and execute code as root. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/new-cpanel-flaw-lets-hosting-account.html))

### 7. WeChat Zero-Click Worm Hijacks Accounts via Incoming Calls
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A zero-interaction, self-propagating account-takeover technique on a major messaging platform is a notable new attack pattern worth tracking for VAPT scenario planning.
Researchers at Calif built a worm that takes over WeChat accounts via an incoming call — the victim need not answer — and demonstrated it spreading across test phones. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/wechat-zero-click-worm-took-over.html))
