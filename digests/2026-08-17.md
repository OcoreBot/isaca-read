# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-08-17

## Top items

### 1. Max-severity SAP Commerce Cloud RCE flaw actively exploited
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A maximum-severity remote code execution vulnerability is being exploited just three days after patching — a critical patch-velocity failure mode auditors should test for in change/vulnerability management controls.
Threat intel firm Defused reports active in-the-wild attacks against unpatched SAP Commerce Cloud instances. Organizations running the platform should verify patch deployment immediately and check for indicators of compromise. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/max-severity-sap-commerce-cloud-flaw-now-targeted-in-attacks/))

### 2. macOS Screen Sharing auth bypass exploited after public exploit code emerges
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Demonstrates how quickly public PoC code turns into real-world exploitation of built-in OS services — a useful case for VAPT teams scoping macOS fleet testing and for CISOs justifying faster patch SLAs.
The Netherlands' NCSC warns attackers are actively exploiting an authentication bypass in macOS Screen Sharing to deploy a Monero cryptominer, following release of public exploit code. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-exploit-macos-screen-sharing-flaw-to-deploy-monero-miner/))

### 3. Shell investigates possible breach after Clop data-theft claims
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A claimed 89GB theft from a critical-infrastructure energy operator raises third-party/supply-chain due-diligence questions and potential regulatory notification obligations pending confirmation.
The Clop ransomware gang claims to have stolen 89GB of data from Shell; the company says it is investigating a "potential incident." No breach has been confirmed yet. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/shell-investigates-potential-incident-after-clop-data-theft-claims/))

### 4. RingCentral breach exposes 1.6 million accounts
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Large-scale exposure of a widely used unified-communications platform creates downstream credential-stuffing and social-engineering risk for every customer organization — a vendor-risk item worth flagging in third-party registers.
The ShinyHunters extortion group stole personal data from 1.6 million RingCentral accounts in a July intrusion, per Have I Been Pwned. Customers should enforce MFA and monitor for account compromise. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ringcentral-data-breach-exposed-info-of-16-million-accounts/))

### 5. Arrests made over €30M bank fraud via service-provider vulnerability
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A textbook example of supply-chain risk crystallizing into financial loss — auditors should use this as evidence for tightening third-party vulnerability management requirements in banking relationships.
Four suspects were arrested in Brazil and three charged in Europe for exploiting a flaw at a service provider to withdraw funds from Commerzbank customer accounts, totaling over €30 million. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-arrested-over-30m-bank-fraud-exploiting-service-provider-flaw/))

### 6. New Evooo1Bot Linux botnet hijacks routers as traffic relays
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A fresh Mirai-based technique for VAPT teams testing edge/gateway device exposure, and a reminder for CISOs to inventory and patch internet-facing routers before they become relay infrastructure for other attacks.
The modular, Mirai-derived Evooo1Bot targets internet-facing gateway devices and converts them into SOCKS5 proxy nodes for relaying malicious traffic. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-evooo1bot-linux-botnet-turns-routers-into-traffic-relay-nodes/))
