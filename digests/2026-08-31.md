# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-08-31

## Top items

### 1. Three CVSS 10.0 ServiceNow AI Platform Flaws
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Unauthenticated, maximum-severity code/SQL injection flaws in a widely-deployed enterprise platform demand immediate patch verification, especially for hosted instances audit teams may assume are already remediated.
ServiceNow released patches for four AI Platform flaws, three rated CVSS 10.0 and exploitable in certain circumstances without authentication. ServiceNow has already pushed the update to hosted instances. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/three-cvss-100-servicenow-flaws-could.html))

### 2. PaperCut Zero-Day Chained for Unauthenticated RCE
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Active zero-day exploitation with a chained bypass of the first patch shows attackers moving faster than vendor fixes; print management is a commonly under-scoped audit area.
PaperCut NG/MF is under active zero-day attack across all versions; a second emergency patch followed within days after researchers found ways to chain two flaws for unauthenticated code execution. Temporary mitigation is IP restriction pending patching. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/attackers-chain-two-papercut-flaws-to.html))

### 3. Anthropic: Infostealers Hijacking Claude Sessions
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A new attack vector where endpoint infostealer malware steals cloud AI session tokens rather than credentials — SIEM/XDR session-anomaly detection and endpoint hygiene should be reviewed.
Anthropic warned that infostealer malware on user devices is stealing active Claude login sessions, letting attackers access accounts and consume victims' usage quota. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-warns-infostealer-malware-is-hijacking-claude-sessions-to-drain-usage/))

### 4. ownCloud Flaw Added to CISA KEV After Nuclear-Sector Breach
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** KEV listing plus confirmed targeting of a sensitive research body raises this above routine patch management for any organization running ownCloud.
CISA added a critical ownCloud vulnerability to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog after a Chinese-speaking threat actor used it to steal nuclear records from a Philippine research body. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/snowflake-github-actions-flaw-lets.html))

### 5. Malicious Chrome/Edge Extensions Stealing Crypto and Browser Data
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Browser extension supply-chain compromise combined with ClickFix social-engineering payloads is a dual-vector risk; audit extension allowlists and user awareness controls.
Multiple Chrome Web Store and Edge extensions were found deploying a malware framework that stole cryptocurrency and browser data while injecting ClickFix lures. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/chrome-web-store-extensions-caught-stealing-crypto-browser-data/))

### 6. Critical cPanel Flaw Enables Root Takeover of Shared Hosts
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** In multi-tenant hosting, one customer could escalate to full server root — a direct tenant-isolation failure relevant to VAPT scoping and audit of hosting providers.
cPanel patched CVE-2026-65643, a flaw in domain parking/addon domain handling that lets one hosting customer execute code as root across a shared server. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/critical-cpanel-flaw-could-let-one.html))

### 7. Over 8,300 Gitea Servers Still Vulnerable to Active RCE Exploitation
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Unpatched, internet-exposed source-code repositories at this scale represent a direct software supply-chain risk; CISOs should inventory Gitea deployments now.
Shadowserver reports more than 8,300 internet-exposed Gitea instances remain unpatched against a critical code-execution flaw under active exploitation. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/over-8-300-gitea-servers-vulnerable-to-code-execution-attacks/))

### 8. APT28-Linked HOOKEDGE Backdoor Targets European Governments
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Nation-state targeting of diplomatic organizations signals elevated geopolitical cyber activity; government/critical-infrastructure CISOs should tune detections to reported TTPs.
Recorded Future's Insikt Group flagged HOOKEDGE backdoor campaigns against government and diplomatic targets in Romania, Spain, and Türkiye, attributed to APT28. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/apt28-linked-hookedge-backdoor-targets.html))
