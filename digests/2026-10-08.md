# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-10-08

## Top items

### 1. Denmark's National CPR Registry Breach Exposes 8.8 Million People
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A third-party account compromise reaching a national identity registry is a textbook third-party access control failure — directly relevant to vendor access reviews and data-residency audits.
Denmark's digitalization ministry confirmed attackers accessed names, addresses, and personal ID numbers for 8.8 million living and dead residents through a private Danish company's account on the Civil Personal Register (CPR). ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/denmark-says-attackers-accessed-cpr.html))

### 2. Microsoft Exchange Out-of-Band Patch for Mailbox-Read Privilege Escalation
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Mailbox access is a top-tier compromise target; an out-of-band emergency patch signals exploitability and makes this a same-day patch-verification item for on-prem/hybrid Exchange estates.
CVE-2026-96940 (CVSS 8.8) lets an authenticated attacker escalate privileges to read other users' mailboxes; Microsoft shipped emergency updates outside its normal cycle. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/microsoft-exchange-flaw-lets.html))

### 3. FBI/Secret Service: FortiBleed Credential Harvesting Still Active, 86,644 Devices Hit
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** An active federal advisory on firewall-admin credential theft is a direct control test — audit should confirm FortiGate management interfaces are not internet-exposed and MFA is enforced.
The FBI and USSS warned the FortiBleed campaign remains active against internet-facing FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN gateways, having harvested credentials from over 86,644 devices. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/fbi-warns-fortibleed-remains-active.html))

### 4. Cisco Nexus 3000/9000 Critical Unauthenticated RCE in OAM Feature
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Unauthenticated RCE on core data-center switching fabric is board-reportable and warrants emergency change-control for NX-OS patching.
Cisco patched roughly nine Nexus 3000/9000 flaws; critical bugs in the OAM feature for VXLAN/MPLS allow remote code execution to anyone who can reach the switch with crafted packets. ([source](https://isc.sans.edu/podcastdetail/10128))

### 5. LMCache Critical RCE in LLM Server Infrastructure — No Patch Available
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** An unauthenticated RCE with no fix forces a risk-acceptance decision for any internal LLM/RAG deployment — a live item for GenAI-governance audits.
A critical flaw in LMCache's multiprocess mode, used to speed up vLLM-based LLM servers, lets an unauthenticated attacker run code on the cache server; no patched version exists yet. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/unpatched-critical-lmcache-flaw-lets.html))

### 6. Compromised ccTLD Registries Yield Fraudulent Certificates for Google Domains
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A registry-level compromise undermining the certificate trust chain should prompt review of any PKI/DNS validation controls that implicitly trust ccTLD-issued certs.
Attackers breached registry operators for Ghana (.gh), Sierra Leone (.sl), and American Samoa (.as) and obtained unauthorized HTTPS certificates for Google domains; Chrome has blocklisted the certificates. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attackers-hijack-gh-sl-and-as.html))

### 7. PoeLLM Malware Compromises 3,400+ Exposed AI Servers for Cryptomining
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Rapid, automated weaponization of exposed LLM inference endpoints is a direct VAPT finding — organizations running public-facing AI services need segmentation and exposure checks now.
A new malware family targets exposed AI/LLM infrastructure (e.g., vLLM, llama.cpp) to deploy cryptocurrency miners and turn compromised servers into scanners and exploit launchpads, with over 3,400 servers hit. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/poellm-malware-infects-exposed-ai-servers-in-cryptomining-attacks/))
