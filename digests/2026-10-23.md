# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-10-23

## Top items

### 1. CISA KEV: Plex2Shell Flaws Actively Exploited, Federal Remediation Due Today
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Four chained Plex Media Server flaws (Plex2Shell) are under active exploitation, and CISA's KEV-driven remediation deadline for two of the four CVEs falls today — a concrete test of whether your vulnerability-management SLA tracking actually works.
CISA added the Plex2Shell CVEs to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on October 20–21 after confirming in-the-wild exploitation. Audit teams should verify Plex instances are patched and pull evidence of KEV-deadline tracking for this cycle. ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/cisa-orders-federal-agencies-to-patch.html))

### 2. eMudhra CA Keys Compromised — Certificate Authority Exits Global SSL/TLS Business
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A certificate authority with recoverable intermediate private keys undermines the trust chain for every certificate it issued; browser vendors had to intervene directly.
Mozilla and Chrome confirmed on October 20 that eight eMudhra intermediate CA keys were compromised, and eMudhra's board voted October 22 to exit the global SSL/TLS business entirely. Go 1.27.3/1.26.10 and major browsers now reject chains through the affected keys. Audit teams should confirm no internal or customer-facing systems rely on eMudhra-issued certificates. ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/emudhra-to-exit-global-ssltls-business.html))

### 3. Arista Warns of Critical VeloCloud SQL Injection Zero-Day Exploited in Attacks
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** SD-WAN orchestrators sit above an entire network fabric; this flaw lets even a read-only authenticated account escalate to full control, which breaks the usual assumption that limited roles limit blast radius.
Arista disclosed on October 22 that attackers are actively exploiting a SQL injection zero-day in VeloCloud Orchestrator (VCO), letting any logged-in user — including read-only accounts — escalate privileges. Patch immediately and audit read-only account activity logs for signs of abuse. ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/arista-warns-of-critical-velocloud-sql.html))

### 4. Two CVSS 9.0 Linux KSMBD RCE Flaws Disclosed Before Kernel Fixes Exist
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Public disclosure of unauthenticated-RCE-capable bugs ahead of a vendor fix is a VAPT team's worst scheduling problem — there's nothing to patch to yet, so compensating controls are the only option.
Two flaws in KSMBD, the Linux kernel's built-in SMB server, were disclosed on public mailing lists October 22 with no mainline fix available; one (ZDI-CAN-33774) can be triggered pre-authentication. Disable or firewall KSMBD on exposed Linux file servers until a fix lands. ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/two-cvss-90-linux-ksmbd-rce-flaws.html))

### 5. ISACA: Agentic AI Benchmarking Gap Leaves BFSI Auditors Without Assurance Evidence
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** This is a direct statement of the problem IS/IT auditors are now facing in the field — vendor claims about agentic AI can't be independently verified against domain-specific regulatory and multilingual risk benchmarks.
ISACA's October 22 analysis argues that general-purpose AI benchmarks don't cover the regulatory, multilingual, and agentic risks banks and financial institutions actually face, leaving an assurance gap auditors can't close with existing tools. It calls for domain-specific evaluation frameworks as a prerequisite for auditable evidence. ([ISACA Now](https://www.isaca.org/go/i-am-isaca))

### 6. China-Linked Storm-0940 Used Agentic AI Pentest Framework in ConnectWise Breach
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Attackers are now using the same class of agentic AI pentesting tools defenders adopt — a preview of what VAPT and threat-modeling assumptions need to account for going forward.
Microsoft disclosed October 22 that China state-aligned Storm-0940 used ARTEX, a Go-based agentic AI penetration-testing framework, in the supply-chain compromise of ConnectWise's Cloud Backup application. CISOs should treat AI-driven attack tooling as a near-term, not theoretical, threat-model input. ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/china-linked-hackers-use-artex-ai.html))
