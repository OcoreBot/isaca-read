# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-10-15

## Top items

### 1. Cisco Secure Email Gateway Zero-Day Actively Exploited, Added to CISA KEV
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A pre-auth, CVSS 10.0 RCE in a perimeter mail appliance with a federal remediation deadline is a direct test of emergency-patch SLAs and email-gateway segmentation controls.
Cisco patched CVE-2026-76519, a path-traversal flaw letting an unauthenticated attacker write files to the appliance OS via a crafted email attachment, after confirming active exploitation. CISA added it to KEV on October 14 with remediation due October 17. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisco-patches-actively-exploited-secure-email-gateway-zero-day/))

### 2. Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS RCE Zero-Day Exploited in Attacks, Added to KEV
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Active exploitation of a firewall RCE, now on the federal KEV list, is an immediate perimeter-control-failure scenario for any network-security audit.
CVE-2026-0322, a high-severity code-injection flaw in PAN-OS, is being exploited against firewalls in the wild. CISA added it to KEV on October 14 alongside remediation guidance due October 17. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/palo-alto-networks-warns-of-pan-os-rce-flaw-exploited-in-attacks/))

### 3. Ahsay AhsayCBS Backup Software — Two Flaws Added to CISA KEV
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Backup infrastructure is a high-value ransomware target; an authentication-bypass plus command-injection pair under active exploitation threatens the integrity of an organization's last line of recovery.
CISA added CVE-2026-105133 (improper authentication) and CVE-2026-105134 (command injection) in Ahsay's AhsayCBS backup platform to KEV on October 14, both with remediation due October 17. ([source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/14/cisa-adds-four-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog))

### 4. Ledger Hardware Wallets Shipped With Hidden LTE Implants, $100M+ Reported Stolen
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Unauthorized cellular modules inserted into a security product through the reseller channel is a textbook supply-chain and vendor-assurance control failure.
Ledger asked resellers across Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand, the Philippines, Vietnam, Malaysia, and Hong Kong to halt sales after finding tampered wallets with hidden LTE/SIM modules exfiltrating seed phrases; losses are reported above $100 million. Ledger is investigating its manufacturing and distribution chain. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ledger-asks-sellers-across-asia-to-halt-sales-after-supply-chain-attack/))

### 5. DOJ Dismantles North Korean IT-Worker Fraud Network, Charges 14
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Fraudulent remote-worker placements bypassing identity and HR vetting controls remain a live insider-threat vector that background-check and remote-access-provisioning audits need to address.
The DOJ raided laptop farms, seized roughly 187 computers, and charged 14 people — a mix of U.S. citizens and Chinese nationals — for enabling North Korea's "Famous Chollima" remote IT-worker fraud scheme. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fbi-raids-dprk-it-worker-laptop-farms-in-texas-us-nationals-arrested/))

### 6. GitLab Patches CVSS 10.0 Unauthenticated RCE
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A maximum-severity, no-auth-required code-execution path in a widely used DevOps platform is a critical finding for any software-supply-chain or CI/CD audit scope.
GitLab shipped patches for a flaw in Community and Enterprise Edition that, under certain conditions, allowed unauthenticated attackers to execute arbitrary code on the server. No public exploitation confirmed yet. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/gitlab-patches-cvss-100-flaw-that-could.html))

### 7. CyberXero Used Claude Code in AI-Augmented Campaign That Stole 628,000 Ukrainian Records
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Threat actors pairing commodity exploitation with AI coding assistants to speed up initial access is a technique VAPT programs should start modeling against.
Researchers disclosed a two-pronged, AI-augmented campaign against WordPress sites, e-commerce platforms, and critical-infrastructure targets in Ukraine, combining commodity offensive tooling with Claude Code to accelerate the operation; over 628,000 records were stolen. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/cyberxero-uses-claude-code-in-campaign.html))
