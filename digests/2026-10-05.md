# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-10-05

## Top items

### 1. Citrix NetScaler SAML Zero-Day Added to CISA KEV
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Actively exploited zero-day with a 3-day federal remediation clock; a textbook case for testing patch-management SLAs and emergency-change procedures.
Citrix issued emergency patches for CVE-2026-88779, a memory-corruption flaw in NetScaler exploited in zero-day attacks; researchers are investigating potential RCE beyond the confirmed DoS impact. CISA added it to the KEV catalog on 2026-10-04 with remediation due 2026-10-07. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/citrix-patches-netscaler-saml-zero-day-exploited-in-attacks/))

### 2. Dell CSM Flaws Give Unauthenticated Admin Access on Kubernetes Nodes
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A perfect CVSS 10.0 score on infrastructure connecting storage to Kubernetes is a high-value finding for any VAPT scope touching container platforms.
Dell patched critical Container Storage Modules vulnerabilities, including CVE-2026-63688 (CVSS 10.0), which let unauthenticated attackers gain admin access and compromise entire Kubernetes clusters. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/dell-csm-flaws-enable-unauthenticated.html))

### 3. FortiMail Zero-Day Exploited for Unauthenticated File Writes
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Email gateways sit on the perimeter; active exploitation of an unauthenticated write/RCE path demands immediate verification of patch status and compensating controls.
CISA added CVE-2026-104286, a path-traversal flaw in Fortinet FortiMail, to its KEV catalog following confirmed exploitation. Fortinet has released patches; disabling identity-based encryption if unused is an interim mitigation. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/critical-fortimail-zero-day-flaw.html))

### 4. GitLab Patches Critical AI Gateway RCE
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** CI/CD and AI-tooling infrastructure is increasingly in scope for supply-chain audits; this flaw lets an authenticated user pivot to command execution on self-hosted gateways.
GitLab disclosed and patched a critical (CVSS 9.9) flaw in its AI Gateway/Duo Agent Platform that allowed logged-in users to run commands on the gateway. Only self-hosted AI Gateway deployments are affected. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/gitlab-patches-critical-self-hosted-ai.html))

### 5. Warlock Ransomware Keeps Weaponizing SharePoint Flaws
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Illustrates control failure beyond patching alone — the actor disables security tooling post-exploitation, relevant to incident-response and defense-in-depth audit questions.
The China-linked Warlock group continues exploiting old and new Microsoft SharePoint vulnerabilities to disable security tools and deploy ransomware against water utilities, telecoms, regional government, and a university in Portuguese- and Spanish-speaking countries. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/warlock-exploits-sharepoint-flaws-to.html))

### 6. TA419 Phishing Campaign Targets U.S. AI Policy Experts
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Adversary-in-the-middle phishing bypasses standard MFA, a key control gap auditors should probe when reviewing credential-hygiene and conditional-access policies.
A newly identified China-nexus group, TA419, is running Microsoft AitM credential-phishing campaigns against AI policy experts at U.S. think tanks, universities, and law firms, impersonating prominent figures. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/china-aligned-ta419-targets-us-ai.html))

### 7. Danish Technical University (DTU) IAM Breach Exposes 200,000 Records
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A breach of the identity and access management system itself — a direct lesson for IAM segmentation and access-logging audits.
DTU disclosed that attackers accessed its identity and access management system and downloaded data on up to 200,000 users. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/danish-university-dtu-breach-exposes-data-of-up-to-200-000-people/))
