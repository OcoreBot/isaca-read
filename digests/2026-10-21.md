# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-10-21

## Top items

### 1. Critical PHP Core Flaws (CVSS 9.2) Patched After Details Leaked Early
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** PHP underpins a huge share of web applications and legacy systems; the fix applies to the language core, not an extension, so exposure can't be scoped by configuration alone.
Two critical vulnerabilities affecting all PHP versions back to 5.x (only 8.x remains supported) were patched October 21 at 14:00 UTC, ahead of schedule after leaked details raised exploitation risk. Audit teams should inventory PHP versions in use and treat this as an emergency patch, not a routine cycle item. ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/php-warns-admins-of-two-critical-flaws-emergency-patches-coming-tomorrow/))

### 2. Eight eMudhra Intermediate CA Keys Compromised, Forcing Browser-Wide Revocation
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Compromise of a certificate authority's signing keys is a foundational trust failure — any certificate chain through these keys can no longer be trusted, regardless of an organization's own controls.
A scripting error during an HSM key ceremony left eight eMudhra intermediate CA private keys effectively one byte long and recoverable; Mozilla, Google, and Apple have revoked the certificates, and Go 1.27.3/1.26.10 now reject them outright. Organizations should check whether any internal or vendor certificates chain to eMudhra and plan for reissuance. ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/eight-emudhra-ca-keys-compromised.html))

### 3. Qilin and INC Ransomware Groups Actively Exploiting PAN-OS/Prisma Access MFA Bypass
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Active exploitation of an MFA bypass on perimeter firewall/VPN infrastructure is a direct path to ransomware deployment, even after a patch exists.
Palo Alto Networks confirmed continued in-the-wild exploitation of CVE-2026-0257, a patched authentication-bypass flaw in PAN-OS and Prisma Access, by Qilin and INC ransomware affiliates. Confirm patch status on all PAN-OS/Prisma Access instances and review firewall/VPN logs for anomalous authentication. ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/qilin-and-inc-ransomware-groups-exploit.html))

### 4. Siemens Software Center DLL-Sideloading Flaw Already Exploited in ClickFix Attacks
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Active exploitation in OT/industrial environments raises the stakes beyond typical IT patch SLAs, and ClickFix as a delivery method continues to mature across unrelated vulnerabilities.
Siemens patched CVE-2026-110936 (CVSS 8.4), a DLL sideloading flaw in Siemens Software Center that attackers are already abusing via ClickFix-style social engineering to drop remote-access trojans. Manufacturing and industrial sites running Siemens tooling should patch immediately and retest ClickFix detection coverage. ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/clickfix-attackers-exploit-siemens.html))

### 5. Public PoC Released for Critical FortiSIEM Unauthenticated RCE
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** SIEM platforms are high-value targets — compromising one can blind detection and forensic capability precisely when it's needed most.
A public proof-of-concept now exists for CVE-2026-106173 (CVSS 9.4), a FortiSIEM path-traversal flaw enabling unauthenticated RCE via port 27918. Patch or isolate the affected port at the firewall as an interim control while patching completes. ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/critical-fortisiem-flaw-gets-public.html))

### 6. CISA Issues #StopRansomware Advisory on SafePay Ransomware
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A fresh federal advisory gives audit and VAPT teams current, named TTPs to validate detection and incident-response playbooks against.
CISA's October 20 advisory details ongoing SafePay ransomware activity targeting businesses and critical infrastructure since August 2024, including intrusion and extortion techniques. Map the published IOCs/TTPs against existing detections and tabletop scenarios. ([CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa26-293a))

### 7. ConnectWise Resets Credentials as Cloud Backup Stays Offline After M365 Tenant Compromise
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** An MSP/tooling vendor incident that reached customer Microsoft 365 tenants is a direct supply-chain exposure for every downstream client, not just ConnectWise itself.
ConnectWise disabled seven compromised application identities and is resetting credentials after attackers gained unauthorized access that led to compromised Microsoft 365 tenants; cloud backup remains offline during the response. Clients should verify whether their tenants were affected and audit cloud access logs and credential rotation. ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/connectwise-resets-credentials-as-cloud.html))
