# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-08-27

## Top items

### 1. CISA Orders Federal Patch of Actively Exploited Citrix NetScaler RCE, Six Flaws Added to KEV
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** NetScaler is a perimeter access-control device in most enterprise architectures; active exploitation with a 3-day federal deadline signals imminent widespread attack and should trigger emergency patch review.
CISA ordered federal agencies to patch actively exploited Citrix NetScaler ADC/Gateway RCE (CVE-2026-8452) by Saturday, one of six flaws added to the KEV catalog this week alongside Linux kernel, SQL Server, and Ajax.NET bugs. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-hackers-now-exploiting-citrix-netscaler-rce-flaw-in-attacks/))

### 2. Boston Scientific Cyberattack Disrupts Global Medical Device Operations
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** An operational-technology incident at a life-critical device manufacturer highlights business-continuity and patient-safety exposure from IT/OT convergence.
Boston Scientific confirmed a cyberattack disrupted IT systems globally, affecting orders and shipments of devices including pacemakers and defibrillators; third-party forensics is underway with no restoration timeline given. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/boston-scientific-says-cyberattack-disrupted-operations-globally/))

### 3. Gitea RCE Actively Exploited, Attackers Dropping Miner-Like Payloads
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** New evidence of post-exploitation payloads confirms real-world impact beyond the KEV listing, reinforcing urgency for source-control patching and CI/CD integrity checks.
Following its 2026-08-25 KEV addition, CISA and researchers now report attackers actively exploiting the Gitea code-injection flaw (CVE-2026-60004, CVSS 9.8) to drop cryptominer-like payloads on self-hosted instances. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/critical-gitea-rce-actively-exploited.html))

### 4. GPUThor Rowhammer Attack Defeats NVIDIA GPU ECC for Root Access
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A hardware-level bypass of the vendor-recommended mitigation is a novel local privilege-escalation path for AI/ML workstations and shared GPU compute — an emerging gap in most risk registers.
Academic researchers disclosed GPUThor, a Rowhammer attack against NVIDIA RTX A6000 GDDR6 GPUs that defeats ECC protections, enabling denial-of-service and privilege escalation to root. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/gputhor-rowhammer-defeats-ecc-on-nvidia.html))

### 5. Critical Keycloak Flaw Allows Unauthenticated Account Takeover via Password Reset
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Identity and access management infrastructure is a foundational control; an unauthenticated full account-takeover path in a widely used open-source IAM server warrants immediate patch verification.
Red Hat and the Keycloak project patched a critical flaw letting an unauthenticated remote attacker force a password reset and take over any user account. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/critical-keycloak-password-reset-flaw.html))

### 6. NovaCookies AitM Toolkit Abuses Genuine Docusign Notifications to Steal Microsoft 365 Sessions
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Abuse of legitimate transactional-email workflows to bypass MFA via session-cookie theft undermines email-filtering and conditional-access controls many programs rely on.
A new adversary-in-the-middle phishing toolkit, NovaCookies, redirects Microsoft 365 sign-ins through genuine-looking Docusign notifications, capturing authenticated sessions and evading typical filtering. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/novacookies-campaigns-abuse-genuine.html))

### 7. Oracle HTTP Server / WebLogic Proxy Plug-in Flaw Actively Exploited
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Unauthenticated access to critical backend data via a common application-server component is an immediate confidentiality risk for Oracle middle-tier deployments.
CISA added a maximum-severity, actively exploited Oracle HTTP Server/WebLogic Proxy Plug-in access-control flaw (CVE-2026-21962) to KEV, with federal remediation due 2026-08-27. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/actively-exploited-oracle-weblogic-flaw.html))

### 8. ATF Confirms "Major Incident" After Qilin Ransomware Breach Claims
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A confirmed breach at a federal law-enforcement agency underscores that ransomware groups' claims against government targets increasingly hold up, relevant for incident-response and third-party assurance planning.
The ATF confirmed a "major incident" after the Qilin ransomware gang claimed to have breached one of its systems. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/atf-confirms-major-incident-after-recent-qilin-breach-claims/))
