# Security & Audit Digest — 2026-09-04

## Top items

### 1. Critical Cisco Nexus 9000 Flaw Allows Unauthenticated Root RCE
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Zero-auth remote code execution as root on core switching infrastructure is a top-tier risk requiring emergency patch validation and change-control review.
Cisco patched a critical flaw (CVSS ~9.8) affecting 10 Silicon One-based Nexus 9000 switches, alongside an IOS XR release bundling 7 CVEs (2 rated 9.8). No authentication is required to execute code as root. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/critical-cisco-nexus-9000-flaw-lets.html))

### 2. Sangoma Switchvox SQL Injection (CVE-2026-9586) Actively Exploited
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Added to CISA's KEV catalog with a federal remediation deadline of 2026-09-05 — a concrete compliance trigger for organizations tracking KEV-driven patch SLAs.
An unauthenticated SQL injection (CVSS 9.3) in the Sangoma Switchvox VoIP platform is being actively exploited to deploy reverse shells without credentials. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/attackers-exploit-critical-switchvox.html))

### 3. SonicWall SMA 1000 Zero-Days Chained in Active Attacks
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** VPN appliance compromise gives attackers a direct path into internal networks; both CVEs are KEV-listed with a 2026-09-05 federal remediation deadline.
Attackers are exploiting an SSRF (CVE-2026-83548, CVSS 10.0) and an OS command injection (CVE-2026-83549) in SonicWall SMA 1000 series appliances, which may form an attack chain. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/attackers-exploit-two-sonicwall-sma.html))

### 4. JFrog Artifactory Auth Bypass Exploited to Forge Admin Tokens
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A compromised artifact repository threatens software supply-chain integrity; attackers with forged admin tokens can poison builds. KEV remediation deadline: 2026-09-05.
CVE-2026-82329 (CVSS 9.8), an authentication bypass in JFrog Artifactory, is being exploited days after disclosure to mint administrative tokens. ([source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-exploit-critical-jfrog-artifactory-flaw-to-forge-admin-tokens/))

### 5. CISA Adds Seven Flaws to KEV Catalog
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Direct input for vulnerability-management audits and patch-SLA tracking; includes AI/LLM infrastructure (LiteLLM) alongside network and web-framework CVEs.
CISA added seven actively exploited vulnerabilities to its KEV catalog on 2026-09-02, including Switchvox, both SonicWall SMA1000 flaws, JFrog Artifactory, BerriAI LiteLLM, Kestra OSS, and Kludex Starlette. ([source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/02/cisa-adds-seven-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog))

### 6. Shai-Hulud Worm Now Scans 469 Credential Locations
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** Widening credential-harvesting scope across CI/CD and cloud tooling raises supply-chain and access-control audit priority for DevOps environments.
GitGuardian reports the Shai-Hulud infostealer worm variant now scans 469 locations spanning developer environments, CI/CD tooling, cloud configs, and AI tooling. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/shai-huluds-reach-just-grew-to-469.html))

### 7. Thomson Reuters C-Track Breach Exposes SSNs and Sealed Court Data
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A control-failure breach affecting sensitive legal/PII data across 11 US states plus international jurisdictions — relevant precedent for data-handling and third-party risk audits.
Thomson Reuters disclosed that an unauthorized party accessed C-Track court case management files in March 2026, exposing SSNs and sealed data tied to courts in 11 states, the US Virgin Islands, and Ontario. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/thomson-reuters-court-software-breach.html))

### 8. FalconFlank PoC: CrowdStrike Falcon Privilege Escalation 0-Day
**Why it matters for audit/CISO/VAPT:** A public PoC for privilege escalation in widely deployed EDR software raises exploitation risk and should prompt vendor-advisory monitoring.
A researcher released FalconFlank, a proof-of-concept for a privilege escalation flaw in CrowdStrike Falcon, with no vendor patch confirmed at publication. ([source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/researcher-releases-falconflank-poc.html))
