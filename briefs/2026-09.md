# Journal & Research Brief — 2026-09

## ISACA Journal

Current issue: **Volume 4, 2026 — "The Dual Nature of Data"**, on protecting data as an asset while managing it as a liability.

- [Moving Beyond Severity Scores: A VEX-Driven Approach to Safety-Critical System Risk](https://www.isaca.org/resources/isaca-journal/issues/2026/volume-4/moving-beyond-severity-scores-a-vex-driven-approach-to-safety-critical-system-risk) — argues for translating software supply-chain artifacts (VEX/SBOM) into actionable risk decisions rather than raw CVSS scores.
- [Avoiding the Traps That Lead to Harmful Models](https://www.isaca.org/resources/isaca-journal/issues/2026/volume-4/avoiding-the-traps-that-lead-to-harmful-models) — how the same AI/analytics systems that support enterprise goals can quietly introduce bias, and what governance catches it early.
- [The Governance Gap: Managing Data When It Leaves the Source](https://www.isaca.org/resources/isaca-journal/issues/2026/volume-4/the-governance-gap-managing-data-when-it-leaves-the-source) — data governance needs to travel with the data once it leaves its system of origin, not stop at the source boundary.

## Standards & frameworks

- **ISO/IEC 27000:2026** (6th edition) published 3 July 2026, replacing the 2018 edition — retitled to foreground "cybersecurity and privacy protection," moves shared terminology into an annex for faster updates, and aligns risk language with ISO 31000. Doesn't invalidate existing ISO/IEC 27001 certificates, but signals the direction 27001/27002 will drift in future revisions — worth a skim now rather than waiting for the certification-standard update. ([source](https://www.iso.org/standard/27000))
- **CIS Controls v8.1** — iterative update to v8 adding a "Govern" security function aligned to NIST CSF 2.0, revised asset-class mappings, and expanded Safeguard/glossary clarifications; no new numbered Controls. ([source](https://www.cisecurity.org/controls/v8-1))
- **SOC 2 / AICPA** — the Auditing Standards Board's attestation-standards project (revising baseline AT-C 105, 205, 210 to tighten evidence and risk-assessment provisions) moved into comment-letter deliberation; proposed effective date is engagements beginning on/after 15 June 2029, so no near-term change to current SOC 2 practice, but it's the shape of things to come. ([source](https://www.brightdefense.com/news/aicpa-advances-2026-attestation-changes-for-soc-2/))
- **COBIT** — ISACA is marking COBIT's 30th anniversary and previewing a refresh later this year: more content delivered digitally plus a new customizable assessment module for identifying governance/management gaps. ([source](https://www.isaca.org/resources/news-and-trends/newsletters/atisaca/2026/volume-8/celebrating-three-decades-of-cobit))
- **PCI DSS** — no new version this month; organizations remain under full enforcement of the 4.0.1 future-dated requirements (targeted risk analyses, expanded MFA) that became mandatory in March 2025.

([source](https://csrc.nist.gov/News/2026/nist-releases-sp-800-213r1-ipd))
- **SP 800-209 Rev. 1** (Security Guidelines for Storage Infrastructure) — draft open for comment through **8 Sep 2026**. ([source](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/drafts-open-for-comment))
- **IR 8613** (Multi-Cloud Architecture Challenges: Security and Compliance Implications) — initial public draft, comments due **5 Oct 2026**; relevant to auditors scoping multi-cloud environments. ([source](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/drafts-open-for-comment))
- **SP 1353** (CSF 2.0 Quick-Start Guide: Using AI for CSF Analysis and Reporting) — draft, comments due **15 Oct 2026**. ([source](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/drafts-open-for-comment))
- Finalized this period: **SP 1347** (CSF 2.0 Informative References Quick-Start Guide, 25 Aug 2026) and **IR 8611** (m-NGAC: Transcending Traditional Database Security Models, 27 Aug 2026). ([source](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications))

## Reports worth reading

Nothing new this month — the major annual reports (Verizon DBIR, Mandiant M-Trends) published earlier this year (May and March 2026 respectively) and ENISA's Threat Landscape (October 2025) remain the current editions; no new major industry report dropped in the past month.

From the **Cloud Security Alliance** (new research, not annual reports):

- [Cryptographic Context Injection: When Encryption Defeats AI Guardrails](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/cryptographic-context-injection-when-encryption-defeats-ai-g) (22 Aug 2026) — shows how encrypting malicious instructions (AES-256-GCM) can slip them past AI content classifiers before the model decrypts and acts on them.
- [Post-Quantum Cryptography Key Management](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/post-quantum-cryptography-key-management) (19 Aug 2026) — key-management guidance for the PQC transition, framed around "harvest now, decrypt later" risk.
- [The State of Hybrid and Multi-Cloud Security Policy Management](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/state-of-hybrid-and-multi-cloud-security-policy-management) (17 Aug 2026) — survey of 515 practitioners on policy consistency gaps across hybrid/multi-cloud estates.

## What to act on

- Skim the CSA cryptographic-context-injection paper before your next AI-system risk assessment — it's a concrete bypass technique worth adding to test scenarios, not just a theoretical concern.
- Note the NIST SP 800-209 Rev. 1 (storage infrastructure) and IR 8613 (multi-cloud) comment deadlines (8 Sep and 5 Oct) if you want to submit input before they finalize.
- Read the ISO/IEC 27000:2026 overview now so the terminology/positioning shift doesn't surprise you when it works through into the next ISO/IEC 27001 revision.
