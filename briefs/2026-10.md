# Journal & Research Brief — 2026-10

## ISACA Journal
Current issue: **Volume 5, 2026 — "The Fundamentals of Trust."** The theme centers on reinforcing privacy, governance, and bias-mitigation practices as the foundation of enterprise trust.

- [Breaking Privacy by Design: An Offensive Perspective on Systems Failure](https://www.isaca.org/resources/isaca-journal/issues/2026/volume-5/breaking-privacy-by-design-an-offensive-perspective-on-systems-failure) — frames privacy failures as an attack surface, useful for red-team-style privacy reviews.
- [AI Augmentation and Audit's Transparency Gap](https://www.isaca.org/resources/isaca-journal/issues/2026/volume-5/ai-augmentation-and-audits-transparency-gap) — examines how AI-assisted audit work creates explainability gaps auditors must disclose.
- [The Role of Foundational Trust in Evolving Access Control](https://www.isaca.org/resources/isaca-journal/issues/2026/volume-5/the-role-of-foundational-trust-in-evolving-access-control) — ties trust models to modern (zero-trust-adjacent) access control design.

## Standards & frameworks
- **PCI Secure Software Lifecycle Standard v2.0** released September 28, 2026 — aligns with Secure Software Standard v2.0, adds a Sensitive Asset Identification Document (SAID) requirement and new provisions for AI/"digital tools" in vendor SDLC processes; relevant to any PAN-handling software vendor assessments ([source](https://blog.pcisecuritystandards.org/pci-ssc-releases-version-2.0-of-the-secure-software-lifecycle-standard)).
- **CIS Benchmarks** September 2026 refresh — 19 revised and 2 new benchmarks; Microsoft Defender Antivirus guidance split into its own benchmark, and Linux benchmarks (AlmaLinux, Oracle, RHEL, Rocky) gained 92 new recommendations ([source](https://www.cisecurity.org/insights/blog/cis-benchmarks-september-2026-update)).
- COBIT, ISO/IEC 27001/27002, and SOC 2 criteria: nothing new this month — SOC 2 activity is limited to AICPA's in-progress 2026 attestation standards project (no final standard issued yet) ([source](https://www.brightdefense.com/news/aicpa-advances-2026-attestation-changes-for-soc-2-2/)).

## NIST watch
- **SP 800-82 Rev. 4** (Guide to Operational Technology Security) — initial public draft posted September 21, 2026; restructured around CSF 2.0 and expanded to cover building automation, water/wastewater, rail, and maritime OT. Comments due **November 30, 2026** ([source](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/09/guide-operational-technology-ot-security-nist-requests-comments-draft-sp)).
- **IR 8623** draft (CSF 2.0 Community Profile for Federal O-RAN deployment) posted September 17, 2026 ([source](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications)).
- **SP 1352** finalized September 16, 2026 — small-business primer for assessing SP 800-171A Rev. 3 (CUI) requirements ([source](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications)).
- **SP 800-209 Rev. 1** (storage infrastructure security guidelines) — comment period already closed September 8, 2026; final expected in a future cycle ([source](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/07/security-guidelines-storage-infrastructure-draft-sp-800-209r1-available)).

## Reports worth reading
- **ENISA Threat Landscape 2026**, published September 22, 2026 — analyzed 8,257 EU incidents from 2025: DDoS (51.3%) and unauthorized access (39.5%) were the top incident types, phishing drove 77.8% of social-engineering intrusions, ransomware remained the most impactful threat, and public administration was the most-targeted sector ([source](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/publications/enisa-threat-landscape-2026)).
- **CSA Zero Trust Program Management Guidance** and **Zero Trust Microsegmentation Guidance**, both released September 8, 2026 — practical guidance for structuring a zero-trust program and implementing fine-grained segmentation controls ([source](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/zero-trust-program-management-guidance)).
- **CSA — Comparing FHE and Other PETs**, released September 28, 2026 — compares fully homomorphic encryption against other privacy-enhancing technologies for data-confidentiality use cases ([source](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/artifacts/comparing-fhe-and-other-pets)).

## What to act on
- Read the SP 800-82r4 draft before any OT/ICS-scoped fieldwork and flag comments by November 30 if client environments touch water, rail, or building-automation systems.
- Update software-vendor assessment checklists for the PCI Secure SLC v2.0 changes (SAID, AI/digital-tools provisions) ahead of client SDLC reviews.
- Use the CSA Zero Trust Program Management Guidance to benchmark any in-flight zero-trust roadmaps against a published maturity structure.
