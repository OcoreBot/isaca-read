## Audit, CISO & VAPT angle
Agentic offensive tooling like ARTEX and the strain on incident response are worth raising with leadership. Ask whether IR retainers have real capacity and whether secrets exposed in repos and AI tool settings files are covered by scanning. For audit clients, test secret management and IR retainer assurances. For VAPT, add NTLM relay and SMB/LDAP signing checks to engagements, and expect AI-assisted attackers to find exposed credentials quickly. The French refrigeration attacks also show why OT and building-systems resilience belongs on risk registers.
